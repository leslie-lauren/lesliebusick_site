the short versionHi, I'm Leslie. 2x healthcare founder and former "finance girly" originally from CA, currently in NYC.

It didn't work, but we took the lessons and applied them into a second company: a B2B SaaS platform offering white-label AI and telehealth to clinics. Both were crazy rides. I had a lot of fun, suffered even more, and learned a few things along the way.

Now I advise, angel invest, build things on the side, and try to be useful to early stage founders.
