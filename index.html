About me

The short version: 2x healthcare founder and former “finance girly.”

The slightly longer version:

I started in finance, then left to start my first company at 26: a DTC women's health platform. It didn't work, but we took the lessons and applied them to a second company, a B2B SaaS platform offering white-label AI and telehealth to clinics. Both were crazy rides. I had a lot of fun, suffered even more, and learned a few things along the way.

Now I advise, angel invest, build things on the side, and try to be useful to early-stage founders. Originally from California, currently in New York.

Here's a look at what I've done, what I'm working on, and how to be useful in each other's orbits.
